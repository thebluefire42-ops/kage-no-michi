    How playstyle changes it

    Single-target DPS. Uptime is the share of time spent attacking (the rest is dodging, ending in a dash). Sloppy aim drifts up to ±14°. Close puts ranged weapons at 3 tiles. Shading is per row.

    What one card adds

    Weapon techniques

    How this was measured

    • Every fight runs the game's own code in headless Chrome (weapons, techniques, imbuements, cards, crits, enemy health scaling, shields), 60 steps a second with no drawing. Each fight is 40 seconds.
    • The player is scripted, not an AI. It plays to a style: how much of the time it attacks, how close it stands, how well it aims, and whether it uses specials. The spread across styles shows how much a weapon depends on how it is played.
    • Enemies never attack and always face you. So the sim does not see defensive value: the Shishi Shield's reflects and Bulwark, healing, dodging, spirits taking hits. Shield-bearers are harder than in real play, because nobody walks around the shield.
    • One weapon per run. Spirits from the Restless Spirit Orb take a constant low chip damage (3 per second at area-1 strength, scaled with enemy damage per level and by the share a spirit really takes), standing in for enemy hits: a spirit of a strong enemy lasts longer. Thrown katanas are picked up again before the next throw (walking around obstacles). Hit-freeze and slow motion are off, since they pause the whole game. Every card is tested over 6 runs.
    • Re-run with node tools/sim/run.mjs all and rebuild this page with node tools/sim/report.mjs.
